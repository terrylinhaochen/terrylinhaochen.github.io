## Follow the decisions across changing roles

Sun Like's biography traces Duan Yongping's education, working life, entrepreneurship, and investing. Read those as different settings with different constraints, not as one uninterrupted success formula. What worked for an operating business may depend on conditions that do not transfer to an investment decision.

## Reconstruct what was knowable then

For a consequential choice in the narrative, separate the information available at the time from the successful outcome visible in hindsight. Ask which alternatives were plausible, what could have gone wrong, and which commitments made a change of direction difficult. This is a reading method, not a claim that the biography answers every question.

## Borrow questions rather than a persona

Biographies can make a person's choices appear more coherent than they felt while living them. Keep the author’s interpretation separate from documented events and your own application. The useful output is a decision question you can test in your circumstances, not an imitation of a successful investor's identity.
