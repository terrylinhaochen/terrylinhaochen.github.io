## Progress begins with explanations

Deutsch places explanatory knowledge at the center of human progress. The opening excerpt asks why humans can understand and influence a universe vastly larger than themselves. The important shift is from cataloging what happens to developing explanations that reveal what else may be possible.

## Ask what the explanation rules out

A practical reading exercise is to compare two accounts of the same event. Can either be adjusted to fit almost any outcome? Which parts carry the explanation, and what criticism would force a real revision? This applies the book's interest in explanation without claiming that a neat story is sufficient evidence.

## Optimism still requires error correction

Read the expansive argument as a commitment to solving problems through knowledge, not a prediction that improvement happens automatically. Specific claims still need criticism, evidence, and workable institutions. The useful next step is to identify one obstacle whose mechanism you do not yet understand, then seek an explanation you can challenge and improve.
